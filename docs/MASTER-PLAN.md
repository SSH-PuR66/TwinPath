## TIER 1 — Near-certain money, act before the twins arrive

### 1. NY Hospital Financial Assistance = birth costs near zero
Every DOH-licensed NY hospital must offer sliding-scale financial
assistance (PHL §2807-k): income under **200% FPL → hospitals cannot
charge out-of-pocket**; 200–300% FPL capped at 10% of the Medicaid rate;
up to 400% FPL qualifies for assistance. Uniform application, **no
filing deadline** — a December birth bill can be addressed months later.
Combined with NY pregnancy Medicaid (mother covered 12 months
postpartum; each baby covered a full year automatically), the twins'
delivery — even NICU time — should cost this family almost nothing.
*First action: confirm Brianna's Medicaid/pregnancy coverage now via NY
State of Health; if any bill ever arrives, request the hospital's
financial assistance application before paying a dollar.*
Sources: health.ny.gov/facilities/hospital/financial_assist · dollarfor.org/state_sheet/new-york · lawhelpny.org

### 2. WIC — categorically eligible during pregnancy, doubles with twins
Pregnancy alone qualifies in NY; income limits (~$59k for household of
4) are far above student income; enrollment in Medicaid/SNAP makes WIC
automatic ("adjunct eligibility"). *First action: call the local WIC
agency this week — pregnant applicants don't wait for the birth.*
Source: health.ny.gov/prevention/nutrition/wic/eligibility.htm

### 3. NY Empire State Child Credit — ~$2,000/yr refundable
$1,000 per child under age 4 for tax years 2025–2027, refundable even
with zero tax liability. Twins born by Dec 31, 2026 = ~$2,000 on the
2026 NY return, plus federal CTC and EITC on top. **The twins' birth
date matters at tax time — December vs January is a real dollar swing.**
*First action: calendar a VITA appointment for early 2027; bring both
SSNs from the hospital paperwork.*
Sources: tax.ny.gov/pit/credits/empire_state_child_credit.htm · governor.ny.gov

### 4. SNAP unlocks the day the twins arrive
Students are normally SNAP-ineligible, but exemptions include: caring
for a child **under 6** (both of you qualify from birth day), working
20+ hrs/wk, or **any work-study participation**. SNAP enrollment then
auto-qualifies WIC and other programs. *First action: apply for
work-study at both schools now (double exemption + income), file SNAP
via myBenefits right after the birth.*
Sources: fns.usda.gov/snap/students · clasp.org

## TIER 2 — Big structural money, apply this year

### 5. Iona Innovation Challenge — $20,000 in prizes, at YOUR school
$10,000 first place, $5,000 second, $3,000 third, +$1,000 Fan Favorite,
via the Hynes Institute for Entrepreneurship. **TwinPath is the entry.**
A working fintech app with live bank integration, an AI proposal system,
and a benefits engine — built by a freshman — against typical
slide-deck competition. *First action: email the Hynes Institute this
semester for the 2026-27 timeline and enter.*
Source: iona.edu → Hynes Institute → Iona Innovation Challenge

### 6. CyberCorps SFS — the $27k/yr reality check
Full tuition + **$27,000/yr stipend** + $6,000 professional allowance
for up to 3 years, in exchange for equal-length government cyber service
(literally the FBI/NSA plan, paid for). **Reality: Iona is NOT a
participating institution.** NY participants: Binghamton, Fordham, NYU,
Pace, RIT, Buffalo. Funds start junior year, applications run through
the school. Options: (a) plan a transfer to Pace/Fordham for junior
year, (b) target SFS for a funded master's after Iona, (c) ask Iona's
CS department whether NCAE-C designation/SFS participation is in
progress. DoD SMART scholarship is the parallel path (full tuition +
stipend + guaranteed DoD job; computer science qualifies).
*First action: one email to your CS advisor asking about NCAE-C/SFS
status; one bookmark of sfs.opm.gov's institution list.*
Sources: sfs.opm.gov · nsf.gov (NSF 26-503) · niccs.cisa.gov · smartscholarship.org

### 7. Brianna's nursing pipeline
Nurse Corps Scholarship (HRSA): tuition + fees + books + monthly
stipend for a shortage-facility service commitment — **2026 cycle
closed; 2027 opens in spring — calendar it.** Meanwhile: DCC's CNA
program (**$1,595, 120 hours**) converts to $20+/hr hospital work that
hospitals often pair with tuition benefits, and makes her a stronger
Nurse Corps applicant. *First action: calendar Nurse Corps for
March 2027; price the CNA program against one semester's earnings.*
Sources: bhw.hrsa.gov/programs/nurse-corps · sunydutchess.edu

### 8. CCAMPIS campus child care — ask both schools
Federal grants fund campus child care for Pell-eligible student
parents — covering infant care, extended hours, even diapers/formula at
centers. Eligibility extends beyond current Pell recipients. *First
action: one email each to Iona and DCC financial aid: "Does the college
hold or plan a CCAMPIS grant, and what campus child-care support exists
for student parents?"*
Source: ed.gov CCAMPIS FAQ 2026

### 9. FAFSA professional judgment — after the birth
Unborn children don't count on the FAFSA; **born children do**. Once the
twins arrive and you provide >half their support, your household size
and independence status can change — potentially unlocking larger Pell.
*First action: the week after the birth, request a "professional
judgment / special circumstances review" at both financial aid offices.*
Source: fsapartners.ed.gov 2026-27 handbook

## TIER 3 — Income engines (choose deliberately)

### 10. UPS part-time: the quiet quadruple-dip
Union part-time from **$21/hr**, healthcare with **$0 premiums** (a
family game-changer), pension, guaranteed 3.5 hrs/day, and **Earn &
Learn up to $25,000 toward tuition** — whether it applies to Iona
specifically must be confirmed with local HR. One job = wage + health
coverage + tuition money + SNAP work exemption. *First action: check
UPS jobs for Mount Vernon/New Rochelle-area hubs; ask HR one question:
"Does Earn & Learn pay toward Iona University tuition?"*
Source: about.ups.com part-time possibilities

### 11. Iona RA role — apply freshman January
RAs require sophomore standing; applications run ~January with a
mandatory fall info session (Oct–mid-Jan). Compensation isn't published
— confirm whether it includes housing. Housing value if so: thousands
per year. Lower bar now: paid Front Desk Attendant roles in Residential
Life, no class-year restriction. *First action: attend the fall info
session; ask directly what RA compensation includes.*
Source: iona.edu residential life student employment

### 12. Cyber competition circuit — signal first, money second
NCL ($45 entry, Fall season Aug 17–Dec 11) pays no cash but feeds the
**ISACA NCL scholarship ($5,000 × 5 winners — 2026 closed, next cycle
~May)** and builds the résumé that wins internships. US Cyber Games is
top-40-nationally selective — treat as aspiration. Per Scholas (NYC)
runs free CISA-funded cyber training/apprenticeships. Bug bounties for
beginners: mostly hype; treat as skill-building, not income. *First
action: register for NCL Fall before Oct 9; complete 25%+ of games to
be ISACA-scholarship-eligible next cycle.*
Sources: nationalcyberleague.org · isaca.org foundation · cisa.gov · uscybergames.com

### 13. Services > products (unchanged verdict)
Digital-safety setup for local families/SMBs monetizes now; the
storefront converts later once an audience exists. Track every service
dollar through the CSV rail or Stripe.

## Watch-outs found by the research
- **Iona tuition remission** helps only full-time *employees* (spouse
  100% undergrad remission exists — relevant only if either of you ever
  took a full-time staff job there). Part-time/student workers: no.
- **Nurses for Our Future (NY)**: closed/awarded — dead end, skip.
- **US Cyber Games**: elite-selective; don't budget on it.
- **NCL**: no direct cash — it's a scholarship/career gateway.
- Anything here can change: every enrollment in the Benefits Radar
  should record the date you verified it.
