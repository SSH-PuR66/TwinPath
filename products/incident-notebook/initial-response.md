## First steps

- [ ] Stay calm; avoid deleting anything that may be evidence
- [ ] Write down what happened while it is fresh
- [ ] Preserve suspicious messages, emails and screenshots
- [ ] From a trusted device, change exposed passwords
- [ ] End unknown or unauthorized sessions
- [ ] Enable or verify multifactor authentication
- [ ] Check account recovery email and phone settings
- [ ] Review recent financial transactions if relevant
- [ ] Contact affected providers through official channels
- [ ] Decide whether professional or legal help is required
